> "…having regard for honourable things, not only in the Lord's sight, but also in the sight of men."  
> — 2 Corinthians 8:21

> **Lord God,**  
> let every word written here be true,  
> and every promise made here be kept.  
> Give us honesty when it would be easier to hide,  
> and humility to admit our mistakes and put them right.  
> Help us guard what people entrust to us — their words, their privacy, their trust —  
> as faithfully as we would want our own guarded.  
> Give us wisdom and fairness in every hard decision,  
> courage to protect the vulnerable,  
> and the restraint never to use our power over others unjustly.  
> Let this work serve the people who use it,  
> and bring honour to You.  
> **Amen.**

*The verse and prayer above are not part of this document and do not change the meaning of any of its terms.*
